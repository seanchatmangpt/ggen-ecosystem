# Provenance validity boundary

CalVer: v26.10.1

Donor: W3C Constraints of the PROV Data Model, Recommendation 30 April 2013.
Canonical specification: https://www.w3.org/TR/2013/REC-prov-constraints-20130430/

Decision: reuse the W3C constraint system for PROV inference, normalization, and validity. Do not create a second provenance-validity semantics.

Semantic delta: none for PROV validity. Local standing and authority remain separate.

Preserved boundaries:
- valid provenance is not proof of truth, authorization, semantic equivalence, or external standing;
- inferred provenance does not grant runtime or consequential authority;
- PROV-O representation alone does not establish PROV validity;
- serialization adjacency does not establish normalized-instance equivalence;
- invalid evidence must not be made valid by weakening imported constraints.

Authority ceiling: evidence validation only.
Runtime authority: none.
Consequential authority: forbidden.

Future executable court: project evidence to PROV, normalize and validate under the W3C constraints, preserve the validation result in the receipt, then independently apply local standing and authority admission.

Minimum falsifier: a locally accepted provenance-valid projection that violates an applicable normative W3C PROV constraint.

Retirement candidate: any bespoke provenance-validity rule fully entailed by the imported constraint system.
