# W3C PROV-O prior-art absorption

- Donor: W3C PROV-O Recommendation, 30 April 2013
- Exact specification: https://www.w3.org/TR/2013/REC-prov-o-20130430/
- Namespace: http://www.w3.org/ns/prov#
- Decision: REUSE
- Canonical owner: ggen-ecosystem
- Authority ceiling: EVIDENCE_ONLY
- Imported DO authority: forbidden

## Existing |= Required

PROV-O already supplies the mature interchange vocabulary for Entity, Activity, Agent, derivation, generation/use, attribution and association. Do not manufacture a competing provenance model. Local receipts may project into PROV-O while retaining local standing and authority semantics as an explicitly separate delta.

## Reverse Chesterton constraints

1. Derivation is not semantic equivalence.
2. Attribution/association is not authorization.
3. Provenance evidence does not establish truth, validity, trustworthiness, technical standing, runtime authority, or DO authority.
4. Application extensions specialize/compose with PROV-O; they do not silently redefine core relations.
5. Adjacent PROV serializations do not justify a second provenance ontology.

## Falsifiers

Refuse a projection if it collapses Entity/Activity/Agent identity classes, treats derivation as equivalence, treats attribution as authorization, loses required PROV relations on replay, or promotes provenance evidence above EVIDENCE_ONLY.

Irreducible delta: NONE for provenance interchange. Receipt/authority concepts remain local unless an exact mapping is separately admitted.
