# Consumed-subject binding court

## Claim

A declared immutable donor SHA does not establish the identity of bytes consumed by a verifier.

## Required boundary

For strict standing-import qualification:

1. the import contract declares an immutable marketplace commit SHA;
2. the verifier observes the Git commit of the supplied marketplace checkout;
3. observed SHA equals declared SHA before semantic qualification;
4. the verification receipt records both declared and observed identities;
5. mismatch is refused as `MARKETPLACE_SUBJECT_SUBSTITUTION`;
6. inability to observe the supplied subject is refused as `MARKETPLACE_SUBJECT_UNOBSERVABLE`.

Semantic conformance, copied files, or path adjacency are not identity equivalence.

## Negative court

Given a marketplace tree whose qualification files are semantically conforming but whose checkout HEAD differs from the declared import SHA, strict verification MUST refuse before donor qualification and MUST NOT emit ALIVE standing.

## Authority

Passing this court establishes provenance binding only. It grants no runtime authority and no imported DO authority.
