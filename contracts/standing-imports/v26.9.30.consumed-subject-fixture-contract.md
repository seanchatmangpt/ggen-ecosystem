# Consumed-subject executable fixture contract

The standing-import verifier MUST be exercised against three isolated Git worktrees of the marketplace donor.

| Case | Observed subject | Consumed pack | Required result |
|---|---|---|---|
| exact | manifest commit | clean | ALIVE |
| adjacent | any other commit | clean | REFUSED(MARKETPLACE_SUBJECT_SUBSTITUTION) |
| dirty | manifest commit | modified under declared pack path | REFUSED(MARKETPLACE_SUBJECT_DIRTY) |

All refusal receipts MUST retain `authority=NONE` and `imported_do_authority=forbidden`.

The fixture MUST construct mutations only in disposable worktrees, MUST NOT mutate the canonical donor checkout, and MUST compare the receipt's `observed_marketplace_sha` to the manifest identity. A future deterministic content digest is secondary evidence and MUST NOT replace Git subject identity.

This contract exists to prevent a passing semantic court from laundering adjacent or locally modified donor bytes into exact-subject standing.
