# DfCM operating model

The optimization target is not file count or architectural minimality. It is **maximum lawful reversible option capital**.

## Preserve
Keep multiple lawful transports, pack closures, execution modes, and profile projections available until evidence forces selection.

## Fence
Do not remove a repository, pack, profile, compatibility edge, or transport merely because a newer path exists. First establish semantic equivalence or record the intentional loss.

## Calculus
Every change is classified over:
- objects
- morphisms
- admission
- closure
- authority
- actuation
- receipt
- replay
- standing

## Exclusions
The graph explicitly refuses:
- ambient execution
- unreceipted actuation
- hook actuation
- projection authority
- inspection-as-execution
- workflow-as-success
- repository-object-as-mounted-tree
- named-receipt-as-verified-receipt

## Falsifier
A crown claim must be easier to falsify than to narrate. `queries/falsifiers.rq` names the initial falsifier surface.

## Extension
The `everything` profile is deliberately a CANDIDATE set. Packs may be promoted into the admitted root only after their gates close together against this exact consumer graph.

## Operationalization
The first executable design space is an exhaustive 3×2 grid over transport, knowledge closure, and ggen execution mode. All eight source candidates remain `CANDIDATE`; no source candidate is pre-authorized.
